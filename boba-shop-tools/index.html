Keep recipe assumptions visible

A boba shop can look well stocked while still being short on the ingredient that limits a popular drink. Record the amount used per cup, compare it with realistic cup volume, and keep the unit consistent. Once demand is visible, supplier lead time and safety stock become easier to discuss. This simple sequence helps separate recipe planning from purchasing without turning a quick calculation into a full inventory system.
