What a useful cleaning schedule should answer

  • What must be completed on every visit?
  • Which tasks happen weekly or monthly?
  • Who is assigned to the work?
  • What changed for this client or property?
  • Which jobs still need follow-up?

Use the schedule as an operations record

The schedule becomes more valuable when it records both planned work and exceptions. That makes recurring service easier to hand off, review and improve without rebuilding the process for every client.
