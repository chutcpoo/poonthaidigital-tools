A simple invoice follow-up rhythm

A useful receivables routine does not start with sending more messages. Start by keeping one reliable invoice ID, recording each payment separately, checking the remaining balance, then reviewing overdue age before deciding the next action.

  1. Enter the invoice once with a consistent Invoice ID.
  2. Record every payment against that same ID.
  3. Review remaining balance and due status.
  4. Use aging and follow-up fields to identify what needs attention.
  5. Record promise-to-pay details when a client gives a date or commitment.
  6. Review the dashboard and monthly summary for the wider pattern.

This keeps the spreadsheet focused on operational follow-up rather than pretending to be an accounting or payment-processing system.
