Unsubscribe from marketing emails

Enter the email address you used for a PoonthaiDigital free starter. This stops optional marketing emails. Transactional messages you explicitly request, such as a fresh starter delivery, may still be sent.

What changes

We record the unsubscribe request and suppress optional PoonthaiDigital marketing where supported by our email provider. You can still use all free web tools without an email address.

If you later request another free starter, that one requested delivery may still be sent because it is transactional. Optional follow-up emails remain off unless you opt in again.
