Subject: Quick reminder - Invoice [Invoice #]

Hi [Client Name],

Just a quick reminder that invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due on [Due Date].

If payment has already been sent, thank you - no action is needed. If not, please let me know if you need the invoice or payment details resent.

Thanks,
[Your Name / Business]
Tip: Include the invoice number, amount and due date so the client does not have to search for the details.

Overdue Invoice Follow-Up

Use after the due date has passed. Ask for a factual status update and expected payment date.

EMAIL TEMPLATE
Subject: Invoice [Invoice #] is overdue

Hi [Client Name],

I'm following up on invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], which was due on [Due Date].

Could you please confirm the payment status or let me know the expected payment date?

If you need another copy of the invoice or payment instructions, I can resend them.

Thank you,
[Your Name / Business]

Second Follow-Up: Ask for a Clear Date

Use when an earlier reminder did not resolve the invoice.

EMAIL TEMPLATE
Subject: Follow-up needed - Invoice [Invoice #]

Hi [Client Name],

I'm following up again regarding invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], originally due on [Due Date].

Please reply with the current payment status and, if payment is still pending, the date you expect it to be completed.

I'm keeping our records updated and want to make sure there is no missing information on either side.

Thanks,
[Your Name / Business]
