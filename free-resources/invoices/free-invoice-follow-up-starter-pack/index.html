Use the pack as a starting point

Start with one open invoice. Confirm the amount still unpaid, check the due date, record the last contact and decide the next review date. Use the wording templates only when they fit your tone and agreement with the client.

Why it is free

The pack is designed to solve a small operational problem on its own. If it gives you enough structure, keep using it. A paid workbook is optional, not required.
