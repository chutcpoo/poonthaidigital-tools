Use this as a starting point, not a universal SOP

Every restaurant has different roles, equipment, food-safety procedures and local requirements. Remove what does not apply, add what your operation actually needs, and keep the forms short enough that people will use them.

Why it is free

A useful solution should not always require a purchase. This starter pack is designed to solve a small, real problem on its own. If it is enough for your operation, keep using it.
