How to use this printable pack

Print the four pages as a complete starter set, or use only the page that matches the shift problem you need to solve today. The pack separates opening, closing, manager review and shift handoff so each page has one job instead of turning into an oversized all-purpose checklist.

Customize before you standardize

Review each line against your real restaurant workflow. Remove tasks that do not apply, add role-specific steps from your own SOPs, and decide who owns each item. Keep the wording short enough that staff can scan the page quickly during a busy shift.

What each page is for

  • Opening: confirm front-of-house, kitchen, POS and pre-service readiness.
  • Closing: reset the operation, record exceptions and prepare for the next shift.
  • Manager Daily: keep staffing, service, stock, cash and follow-up visible without duplicating staff task lists.
  • Shift Handoff: pass unfinished work, stock issues, equipment problems and next priorities to the incoming team.

These pages are general operations aids, not universal restaurant SOPs. Add the food-safety, employment, security, maintenance and regulatory procedures required for your business and location.
