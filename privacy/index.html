Privacy

This page explains how PoonthaiDigital Tools handles calculator activity, campaign attribution, starter-download email addresses and optional marketing consent.

Free web tools

The calculators and checklist builders can be used without creating an account or entering an email address. Values entered into calculator fields are used to produce the on-page result and are not intentionally included in our analytics events.

Campaign attribution

When you arrive through a campaign link, the website may read campaign parameters such as source, medium, campaign and content. During the current browser session, the site may store the first campaign attribution in session storage so later tool-use, starter-request and Etsy-link events can remain associated with the original visit even after you move between pages. This session information is designed not to include calculator input values.

Free starter downloads

If you choose to request a reusable starter file, we collect the email address you submit, the starter requested, the source page, basic campaign parameters when present, the time of the request and whether you separately opted in to marketing. We use this information to provide the requested file, operate the download funnel, prevent abuse and understand which resources are useful.

The starter download link is signed and time-limited. Requesting a starter does not require agreeing to marketing.

Optional marketing

Marketing consent is optional and is separate from requesting a free starter. If you check the marketing box, PoonthaiDigital may send practical small-business tips and occasional information about related templates. You can unsubscribe at any time from an email or through our unsubscribe page.

Email, hosting and data processors

We use Resend to deliver email and manage email suppression, Neon to store lead and consent records, and Vercel to host the website and server functions. These providers may process the technical data necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising measurement

We use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to understand page visits and limited application events such as which free tool was used, when a starter was requested and when a visitor clicks a PoonthaiDigital Etsy link. We also use the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms for site-wide PageView measurement and limited funnel events such as ToolUsed, Lead, StarterDownload, MarketingOptIn and EtsyClick so we can understand traffic from Facebook and related campaigns. Meta measurement events are designed not to include submitted email addresses or calculator input values.

External links

Links to Etsy open an external website. Once you leave PoonthaiDigital.com, Etsy's own privacy and data practices apply.

Unsubscribe requests

When you unsubscribe, we may keep the email address on a suppression record so we can honor the request. A later starter file you explicitly request may still generate the one transactional delivery needed to provide that file, but optional marketing remains subject to your consent and suppression preference.

Changes

We may update this notice when website features or service providers change. Last updated: September 18, 2026.
