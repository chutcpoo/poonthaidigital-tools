Why this matters for cash-flow visibility

Revenue on an invoice is not the same as cash already received. A small business can look busy while still having a growing amount of money tied up in unpaid balances. A practical receivables workflow makes four facts visible: what was billed, what has been paid, what remains unpaid and what needs attention next.

Use aging as a prioritization signal, not as the whole process

Aging tells you how old an unpaid balance is. It does not replace the invoice history, the payment log or the next follow-up action. The useful workflow connects all four so an older balance can be reviewed without losing the payment history or client context.
