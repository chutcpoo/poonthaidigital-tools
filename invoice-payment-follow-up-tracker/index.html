BUILT FOR SERVICE BUSINESSES

Know what is unpaid, overdue and due for follow-up

This workbook is designed for freelancers, consultants, independent service providers and small service businesses that invoice customers and manually record payments.

OutstandingSee the remaining balance after recorded payments.
OverdueTrack days overdue and aging buckets.
Next actionUse follow-up status, promise-to-pay and next-action guidance.

7 connected tabs

  • START HERE — workflow guidance and product boundaries.
  • SETTINGS — follow-up and due-soon settings.
  • INVOICES — invoice dates, due dates, amounts, paid amount, balance, status and aging.
  • PAYMENTS — separate payment records linked to Invoice ID.
  • AGING & FOLLOW-UP — overdue visibility, contact status, promise-to-pay, next follow-up and next action.
  • MONTHLY SUMMARY — invoiced amount, payments received and net movement by month.
  • DASHBOARD — outstanding, overdue and follow-up KPIs.

Partial and multiple payments

Each payment is recorded separately in the PAYMENTS tab and linked to the matching invoice by Invoice ID. The workbook totals those payments and recalculates the remaining balance.

Important boundaries

This is an operational tracking workbook. It does not send invoices, send automatic reminders, process payments, connect to bank feeds, provide CRM automation, replace accounting software, or provide tax, legal, accounting or collections advice.
