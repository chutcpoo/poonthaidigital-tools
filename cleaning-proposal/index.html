Core sections for a cleaning proposal

  • Client and property information
  • Service goals and current needs
  • Scope of work
  • Service frequency and timing
  • Pricing and assumptions
  • Optional services and exclusions

Proposal is not the same as a contract

A proposal explains the recommended service and commercial offer. Legal terms, liability, cancellation policies and jurisdiction-specific requirements may require a separate agreement reviewed for your business.
