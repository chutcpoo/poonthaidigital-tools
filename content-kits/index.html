FREE FRAMEWORK

Five post types most local businesses can use

Use these before buying anything. The goal is to make your content useful enough that a customer understands what you do and what to do next.

1 · Offer

One real offer

State the actual service, actual timing and actual conditions. Avoid fake urgency or invented savings.

2 · Proof

One real result

Use a real customer review, permissioned before/after or a real process photo. Do not fabricate customer outcomes.

3 · Education

Answer one question

Turn a common customer question into a short FAQ, checklist or quick tip that reduces uncertainty.

4 · Process

Show how booking works

Explain inquiry, quote, scheduling and service steps in plain language.

5 · CTA

Ask for one next step

Choose one action: request a quote, check availability, save the tip, leave a review or ask a question.
