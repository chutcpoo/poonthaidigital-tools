About PoonthaiDigital

PoonthaiDigital creates practical digital operations tools for small businesses, with a focus on simple calculators, checklists and spreadsheet-based workflows.

Our approach

We start with a narrow operational problem and build the smallest useful tool around it. Free website tools are intended for quick planning decisions. When a business needs a repeatable system, the site can point to a matching PoonthaiDigital template sold through our Etsy shop.

Who the tools are for

The current collection focuses on restaurants, cafes, boba shops, bakeries and similar owner-operated businesses. Examples include inventory reorder planning, bakery production capacity, boba ingredient usage and restaurant opening or closing routines.

What we do not claim

These tools are planning aids, not professional advice. They do not replace accounting, legal, tax, food-safety, regulatory, engineering or other specialist guidance. Business owners should adapt workflows to their own operation and applicable requirements.

Where paid products are sold

PoonthaiDigital digital products are currently sold through the PoonthaiDigital Etsy shop. Etsy handles checkout and digital delivery for products purchased there.

How the free site and Etsy shop work together

PoonthaiDigital.com is the free learning and planning layer. It gives visitors focused tools that can be used before buying anything. The Etsy shop is the commerce layer for customers who want a reusable digital product. Keeping those roles separate makes it possible to test whether a free tool is genuinely useful before asking a visitor to consider a paid template.

How we improve the collection

We review how people reach the site, which tools are used and which pages lead visitors toward a matching product. We use those signals to improve the usefulness of the free tools and to decide which business workflows deserve deeper resources next.
